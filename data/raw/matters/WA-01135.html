Vault Buyer, Inc., which is controlled by funds managed and/or controlled by Vista Equity Partners Management, LLC (together with its affiliates, Vista), will acquire, pursuant to an equity purchase agreement, 100% of the equity interests in Nomad CM LLC, IdenTrust, Inc., ASSA ABLOY Access AB and ZeroSSL GmbH (together, the Target Group) from HID Global Corporation, ASSA ABLOY AB and ASSA ABLOY Holding AB (the Acquisition).

Vista is a US-based investment firm specialising in investing in and growing enterprise software, data and technology-enabled businesses, investing through private equity, permanent capital, credit and public equity investment strategies through its fund vehicles.

The Target Group is a provider of digital trust software, including enterprise public key infrastructure (PKI), public trust, Secure Sockets Layer/Transport Layer Security (SSL/TLS) and credential management services, supplied through four business lines: IdenTrust (public trust certificate authority), HydrantID (PKI-as-a-Service), ZeroSSL (self-service SSL/TLS certificates) and ActivID (credential management). The Target Group is currently ultimately owned by ASSA ABLOY AB.

The Target Group’s primary business is concentrated in the United States. It does not have any subsidiaries, employees or premises in Australia. On completion, the Target Group will be solely controlled by funds managed and/or controlled by Vista.
