AVC Operations Pty Ltd (AVC) proposes to acquire substantially all of the assets of the licensed hotel business trading as King O’Malley’s (King O’Malley’s) from King O’Malley’s Pty Ltd, including the exclusive right to carry on the business in succession of the Vendor, under an Asset Sale Agreement (the Acquisition).

King O’Malley’s is a licensed hotel venue located at 131 City Walk, Canberra ACT 2601. It is a typical Australian pub/hotel offering with a public bar, bistro, and function rooms.

AVC is a subsidiary of Australian Venue Co. Holdings Limited which operates over 200 licensed venues throughout Australia and New Zealand, primarily pubs, as well as some bars, clubs and licensed restaurants, and hotel/motels with accommodation services. AVC’s venues are individually branded and are mostly located in inner-city and metro areas, offering a range of products and services including food, alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages, function rooms, live entertainment and gaming facilities.
