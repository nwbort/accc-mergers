Respect Group Limited (Respect Group) proposes to acquire 100% of the business, land and assets of Clifton Co-op Hospital Ltd (CCH) (the Acquisition).

Respect Group is a not-for-profit registered charity and unlisted public company based in Tasmania, which provides residential aged care, retirement living and support at home services. Respect Group owns and operates 47 residential aged care homes across Tasmania, New South Wales, Victoria, Western Australia, Queensland and South Australia. Respect Group operates support at home services in Tasmania, Western Australia, New South Wales and Victoria.

CCH is a member-owned co-operative based in Clifton, Queensland, governed by the Co-operatives National Law. CCH operates a residential aged care and provides support at home services and community housing services, in Clifton, Queensland.

Each of the parties provides funded residential care services and support at home in accordance with the Aged Care Act 2024 (Cth), although Respect Group does not provide support at home services in Queensland. CCH also provides community housing in Queensland. 

Following completion of the Acquisition, Respect Group will continue to operate the services previously provided by CCH.
