Vista Equity Partners Management, LLC (Vista) is a US-based investment firm specialising in investments in enterprise software, data and technology-enabled businesses, investing through private equity, permanent capital, credit and public equity investment strategies through its fund vehicles. 

Quantios Limited (Quantios) is a subsidiary of Tanqueray Topco Limited. Quantios is headquartered in the United Kingdom and is a global provider of Software as a Service (SaaS) and digital solutions for the corporate trust and fund services industry. Its products facilitate operating activities across entity management, document management, regulatory filings & anti-money laundering, client accounting, and practice management. 

Vista, via its subsidiary Queso Bidco Limited, proposes to acquire 100% of the issued share capital of Tanqueray Topco Limited.
