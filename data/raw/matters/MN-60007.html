Craig Mostyn Holdings Pty Limited (together with its subsidiaries, Craig Mostyn), via its wholly owned subsidiary Craig Mostyn Farms Pty Ltd, proposes to acquire 100% of the issued shares in Patmore Feeds Pty Ltd (Patmore Feeds) (the Acquisition).

Craig Mostyn is an Australian food and agribusiness company. Its primary activities include animal husbandry and meat processing of cattle, pigs and sheep in Western Australia. Craig Mostyn owns and operates pig farms across Western Australia and acquires pigs from third parties to produce pork at its pork abattoir and processing facility in Wooroloo, Western Australia. Craig Mostyn’s meat products are sold under the Amelia Park, Linley Valley Pork and V&V Walsh brands. Craig Mostyn’s other businesses include Talloman, Craig Mostyn Protein Trading and Jade Tiger Abalone brands.

Patmore Feeds is a Western Australia‑based producer of pelletised feed for livestock including sheep, cattle (beef and dairy), and pigs. It primarily supplies livestock producers located within Western Australia.

Craig Mostyn and Patmore Feeds both operate in the cattle, pig and sheep meat supply chain. Patmore Feeds is an existing and potential supplier of livestock feed which is used as an input in Craig Mostyn’s animal husbandry and subsequent processing operations.
