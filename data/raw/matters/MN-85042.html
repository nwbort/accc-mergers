Description

VC Operations (WA) Pty Ltd (together with its associated entities, Vets Central) proposes to acquire 100% of the business assets (both tangible and intangible) of Vital Vet

VC Operations (WA) Pty Ltd (together with its associated entities, Vets Central) proposes to acquire 100% of the business assets (both tangible and intangible) of Vital Vet Animal Hospital from Kinsedo Pty Ltd as trustee for the Ellenvale Veterinary Trust (the Acquisition).

Vital Vet Animal Hospital is a full-service veterinary practice in Ellenbrook, Western Australia. The business provides a range of first opinion core and ancillary veterinary services for household pets (extending to exotic pets) in the local area.

Vets Central is a veterinary group that owns and operates 88 first opinion veterinary practices across Australia and New Zealand. In Australia, Vets Central’s practices provide core and ancillary veterinary services for household pets, as well as livestock and other exotic species. The services primarily include routine, planned, and emergency veterinary services (excluding after-hours services). Vets Central owns the Swan Valley Vet Centre, located approximately 9.8km driving distance away from Vital Vet Animal Hospital in West Swan.
