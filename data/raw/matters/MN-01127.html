CVS Vets (Australia) Proprietary Limited, which is part of CVS Group Plc (together, CVS Group), proposes to acquire the business and assets of Karrinyup Small Animal Hospital (KSAH), a small animal veterinary practice located in the suburb of Balcatta in Perth, Western Australia (the Acquisition). 
CVS Group is an integrated veterinary services provider based in the United Kingdom and listed on the London Stock Exchange. In Australia, CVS Group owns a number of small animal veterinary practices in Queensland, New South Wales, the Australian Capital Territory, Victoria, South Australia and Western Australia. CVS Group currently owns five veterinary practices in Perth: Wattle Grove Veterinary Hospital, Mosman Park Veterinary Hospital, North Perth Veterinary Centre, Grantham Street Veterinary Clinic and Dalkeith Veterinary Clinic. 
CVS Group and KSAH both supply small animal veterinary services in Perth. Small animal veterinary practices attend to small animals (such as cats, dogs, guinea pigs, birds, rabbits and other pets), rather than larger animals (such as cattle, sheep and horses), and offer services such as consultation, general and preventative health care/advice, surgery, microchipping, dentistry, radiology, pathology, pharmacy and related services such as puppy school.
