Woolworths Group Limited’s (Woolworths) wholly-owned subsidiary, Fabcot Pty Ltd (Fabcot), proposes to acquire a freehold interest in vacant land located at 38 Ford Road, Parts 219, 221–229 & 231–237 Numurkah Road, 10 Ford Road, and 5 Grant Court, in Shepparton, Victoria (together, the Target Site) from North Shepparton Commercial Pty Ltd and Ford Rd Enterprises Pty Ltd (the Acquisition).

Woolworths is an ASX-listed retailer operating a national supermarket network, including full-line Woolworths Supermarkets, Woolworths Metro outlets and online grocery services, together with other retail and consumer businesses.

Fabcot acquires and develops non-residential property for the Woolworths Group’s retail network. Fabcot intends to develop a shopping centre at the Target Site.

The shopping centre will include a full-line supermarket and ‘Online Hub’ (to be operated by Woolworths), a discount supermarket, specialty retail and pad sites, car spaces and other amenities.
