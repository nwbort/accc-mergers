Description

Petalite Investment Pte. Ltd.

Petalite Investment Pte. Ltd. (Petalite) proposes to acquire approximately 32% of the equity interests in Cinven Discovery Holdings Limited Partnership and its subsidiaries (CD Holdings) (the Acquisition).

The acquirer, Petalite, is an investment holding company and an affiliate of GIC Pte. Ltd. (GIC). GIC is a private limited company established in 1981 under the Singapore Companies Act to manage Singapore’s foreign reserves. 

The target, CD Holdings, is majority indirectly owned by CD Aggregator Limited Partnership (CD Aggregator), which is ultimately controlled by Cinven Limited. CD Aggregator and Cinven Limited are part of Cinven, a private equity firm. 

Cinven and management indirectly hold 100% of the Envu Group (Envu). In Australia, Envu is active in the development and supply of weed, disease and pest control products for non-agricultural applications, including turf and ornamentals, professional pest management, industrial vegetation management, forestry, mosquito management, and rangelands and pasture management.
