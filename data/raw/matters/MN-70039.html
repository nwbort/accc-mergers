Description

Architectus Services BidCo Pty Ltd (Architectus) proposes to acquire 100% of the shares in SBA Architects Pty Ltd (SBA) (the Acquisition).

Architectus Services BidCo Pty Ltd (Architectus) proposes to acquire 100% of the shares in SBA Architects Pty Ltd (SBA) (the Acquisition). Both Architectus and SBA provide architectural services and ancillary interior design services.

Architectus is a multi-disciplinary practice which provides architecture, interior design and wayfinding services. Architectus operates in all Australian states and territories with nine studios located in New South Wales, Queensland, Victoria, Western Australia and the Australian Capital Territory. 

SBA is an architectural services firm which provides masterplanning, architecture and interior design services to clients in the industrial and commercial sectors. SBA has two studios in Australia and operates in New South Wales, Queensland and Victoria.
