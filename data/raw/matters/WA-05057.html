St Ives Gold Mining Company Pty Limited (St Ives) proposes to acquire a single mining tenement and four parcels of freehold land near Kambalda, Western Australia from BHP Nickel West Pty Ltd (BHP Nickel West) by way of an asset purchase agreement (the Acquisition).

St Ives is an indirect subsidiary of Gold Fields Limited (Gold Fields), a South African-based gold mining company with nine operating mines located in Australia, Chile, Ghana, Peru, Switzerland and South Africa, and a major development project in Quebec, Canada.

Gold Fields’ shares are listed on the Johannesburg Stock Exchange and its American depositary shares trade on the New York Stock Exchange. The Gold Fields corporate group (which includes St Ives) has operated in Australia since the acquisition of the St Ives and Agnew gold mines in the Goldfields region in Western Australia in 2001.

The Gold Fields corporate group operates the Gruyere Gold Mine, St Ives Gold Mine, Agnew Gold Mine, and Granny Smith Gold Mine in Western Australia.

BHP Nickel West, an indirect subsidiary of BHP Group Limited (BHP), operated an integrated nickel mining business in Australia. BHP Nickel West’s operations are currently suspended and have been so for approximately 2 years. 

BHP is a global resources company listed on the ASX. BHP extracts and processes commodities including iron ore, coal, and copper.
