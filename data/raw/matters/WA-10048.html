Description

TA Associates Management, L.P.

TA Associates Management, L.P. (TA Associates), via Anchor Bidco B.V., proposes to acquire MedEnvision International BV and its subsidiaries (MedEnvision) by way of a share acquisition. As part of the acquisition, existing shareholders in MedEnvision will rollover a portion of their existing shareholding to retain minority stakes.

TA Associates, headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts, USA, is a private equity firm investing in companies across five target industries: technology, financial services, healthcare, consumer, and business services. 

MedEnvision is a Belgian medical device company headquartered in Westerlo, Belgium. MedEnvision carries on the business of development, manufacturing, distribution, marketing and sale of medical products used to improve the efficiency and effectiveness of operations in the field of orthopaedics.
