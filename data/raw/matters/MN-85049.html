Description

APEM Group Pty Ltd (APEM) proposes to acquire 100% of the issued share capital in Resource Strategies Pty. Ltd.

APEM Group Pty Ltd (APEM) proposes to acquire 100% of the issued share capital in Resource Strategies Pty. Ltd. (Resource Strategies) (the Acquisition).

APEM is a Victoria-based environmental consulting company that provides services to clients in Australia, across a number of sectors including mining (coal and critical minerals), renewable energy, infrastructure and the built environment. APEM and its subsidiaries (AARC, Attexo and Biosis) provide environmental consulting services primarily relating to planning and approvals, environmental management, ecology and heritage matters.

Resource Strategies is a Queensland-based environmental and regulatory consulting company that provides services to clients in Australia, primarily in the mining sector (coal and critical minerals). Its services include major approvals coordination, strategic advisory and due diligence services, operational support and compliance assistance, and rehabilitation and closure support.
