Description

Sumitomo Metal Mining Co., Ltd., acting through its wholly-owned subsidiary SMM Perth Pty Ltd (SMM Perth) (collectively, SMM) entered into an Asset Sale Agreement

Sumitomo Metal Mining Co., Ltd., acting through its wholly-owned subsidiary SMM Perth Pty Ltd (SMM Perth) (collectively, SMM) entered into an Asset Sale Agreement with Rio Tinto Winu Pty Limited (Rio Tinto) to acquire a 30% interest in Rio Tinto’s copper-gold project in Western Australia known as the Winu Project and other assets, including interests in tenements connected to the Winu Project (the Acquisition).

The ACCC previously provided a letter to SMM on 3 July 2025 in relation to SMM’s proposed acquisition of the 30% interest in the Winu Project which had the effect, pursuant to section 189 of the Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (the Act), that Division 2 of Part IVA of the Act would not apply to the acquisition if it was put into effect within 12 months. This 12-month period has now passed, and SMM considers that certain steps contemplated by the Acquisition remain to be completed after the date of this determination.

The Winu Project is a copper-gold deposit located in the northern Pilbara region of Western Australia. The Winu Project is currently under development but not yet in operation. While the Winu Project is primarily a copper project, there is also potential for gold recovery.
