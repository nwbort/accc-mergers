Lighthouse Service Partners Holdco Pty Ltd, through its subsidiary, Lighthouse Service Partners Bidco Pty Ltd (together, Lighthouse) proposes to acquire 100% of the issued share capital in Proven Air Conditioning Pty Ltd (Proven Air).

Lighthouse’s investment structure focuses on acquisitions and investments in home services businesses across plumbing, heating ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC), electrical and related trades in Australia. Lighthouse is ultimately owned by Alpine Investors, a private equity firm headquartered in San Francisco, California.

Lighthouse owns the following home services businesses operating across Australia: BPD Group, Fallon Solutions, Plum, Ken Hall, The Plumbing Doctor, Tonkin, Hilton Tradies, Precise Services Group and Emu. Lighthouse also owns Network Plumbing, which operates in the Sydney and Wollongong region and provides a diversified range of home services, including plumbing, electrical, HVAC and equipment supplies.

Proven Air provides HVAC and electrical services to residential, commercial and real estate customers across Sydney.
