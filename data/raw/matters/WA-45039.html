Description

Paine Schwartz Partners UK Holdings VI, Ltd.

Paine Schwartz Partners UK Holdings VI, Ltd. (PSP VI) proposes to acquire additional ordinary shares in AMP Grapes Investments Limited (AMP) (the Acquisition). The shares will be acquired from AM Fresh Group UK Limited (AM Fresh UK) and from Paine Schwartz Partners UK Holdings V, Ltd. (PSP V), an affiliate of PSP VI. AMP’s only investment is its shareholding in Bloom Fresh International Holdco Limited, the holding company of the Bloom Fresh group.

PSP VI and PSP V are investment holding vehicles of funds managed or advised by Paine Schwartz Partners, LLC (PSP), a private equity firm specialising in sustainable food chain investing. Both are existing shareholders in AMP.

AMP is a UK holding company, majority-owned by AM Fresh UK, part of the AM Fresh group, a Spain-headquartered fresh produce and plant genetics group.

Bloom Fresh develops proprietary table grape varieties and licenses them to growers around the world. In Australia, it operates through Bloom Fresh Aust Pty Ltd, which licenses table grape varieties to Australian growers and provides related agronomic and technical support from Mildura, Victoria.
