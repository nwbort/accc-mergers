Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Limited (Sun Pharma), through a wholly owned subsidiary Sun Pharma America, Inc., proposes to acquire 100% of the shares of Organon & Co. (Organon) (Acquisition).

Sun Pharma is a global specialty generics pharmaceutical company present in over 100 countries. Sun Pharma's portfolio includes cardiology, psychiatry, neurology, gastroenterology and dermatology. In Australia, Sun Pharma is involved in the supply and distribution of drugs across (i) patented medicines, (ii) retail/hospital Sun Pharma branded generics, and (iii) white label generics.

Organon is a global healthcare company present in 140 countries and territories. Its portfolio is focused on women's health, biosimilars, and general medicines.

In Australia, the parties overlap in the supply of statins, selective serotonin agonists for migraine treatment, atypical antipsychotics, antidepressants and mood stabilisers.

The parties also overlap in the supply of antigonadotropin-releasing hormones, specifically in the supply of ganirelix acetate (ganirelix), a gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) antagonist used in in vitro fertilisation (IVF) protocols to prevent premature ovulation.

  • Organon supplies Orgalutran, the originator ganirelix product, which went off patent in Australia in 2018. Orgalutran is manufactured by a third party and distributed in Australia by Alphapharm Pty Ltd.
     

  • Sun Pharma was the first to enter the generic ganirelix market in Australia in 2021 with Ganirelix SUN.

With respect to the supply of ganirelix, Sun Pharma has expanded its existing global ganirelix outlicencing arrangement with Ferring Pharmaceuticals B.V. (Ferring) to Australia (the Ferring Agreement). Under this arrangement, Sun Pharma has agreed to grant exclusive commercialisation, distribution, marketing and sales rights for Ganirelix SUN in Australia to Ferring. Ferring will take over the commercialisation activities of Ganirelix SUN from 1 December 2026 after the transfer is completed. Sun Pharma will continue to manufacture Ganirelix SUN to supply to Ferring. Sun Pharma has provided a summary document of the Ferring Agreement which is attached below.

Sun Pharma also has a pipeline cetrorelix acetate product, which has applied for PBS listing and is a GnRH antagonist.
