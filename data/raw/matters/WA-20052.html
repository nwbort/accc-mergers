Home Timber & Hardware Pty Ltd (HTH), a subsidiary of Mitre 10 Australia Pty Ltd and ultimately of Metcash Limited (Metcash), proposes to acquire from Camden Joinery Pty Ltd, the business of the Picton Mitre 10 hardware store (Picton Mitre 10), together with some stock, plant and equipment from a separate store, Camden Mitre 10 (the Acquisition).

Picton Mitre 10 is located at 4 Henry Street, Picton, NSW. It supplies hardware products to the do-it-yourself (DIY) and trade customer segments. Picton Mitre 10 is a wholesale customer of Metcash.

Camden Mitre 10 is located on the corner of Exeter and Macquarie Grove Road in Camden, NSW. However, this store permanently closed on 31 October 2026, and only some of its plant, equipment and stock will be acquired by HTH. 

Metcash is an ASX-listed wholesaler and distributor of food, liquor, hardware, tools and home improvement products. Its Total Tools & Hardware Group pillar supports independent and company-owned stores operating under Mitre 10, HTH, Total Tools, Thrifty-Link Hardware, True Value Hardware and Hardings brands. Relevantly, Metcash owns and operates Hudson Mitre 10 Trade Centre Leumeah in NSW which services trade customers only (and does not have a consumer / DIY hardware offering).
