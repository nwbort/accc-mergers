Oak UK Bidco Limited (Acquirer) proposes to acquire the entire issued share capital of Blazing Star Boots Superco (Jersey) Limited (Boots) from The Boots Group Limited (Vendor) (the Acquisition).

Prior to the Acquisition completing, Boots will be subject to a reorganisation with the effect that the Vendor will retain part of the Boots business that operates in Mexico and Germany.

On completion of the Acquisition, the Acquirer, which is a special purpose vehicle, will be jointly owned and controlled by Wittington Investments, Limited (Wittington Investments) and Fairfax Financial Holdings Limited (Fairfax).

Wittington Investments is the private holding company for the Weston family based in Canada. Wittington invests globally through a diversified portfolio of direct and fund investments.

Fairfax Financial is a financial holding company based in Toronto, Canada. Through its portfolio companies, Fairfax focuses mainly on property and casualty insurance, reinsurance and the associated investment management.

Boots, and its associated business lines, is a health and beauty retail business with its main office in London and its headquarters in Nottingham, United Kingdom. Boots serves customers and patients through a combination of owned businesses, equity method investments and franchise-owned locations across a diverse range of markets.
