Woolworths Group Limited (Woolworths), via its subsidiary, Fabcot Pty Ltd (Fabcot), proposes to acquire a freehold interest in 38 Lakeview Street, Mariginiup, Western Australia (the Target Site) (the Acquisition). The Target Site is located in a mixed urban development / rural area, is currently used as a residential dwelling, and is approximately 8.22 hectares in size.

Woolworths proposes to construct a new shopping centre at the Target Site, anchored by a full-line Woolworths supermarket, which is anticipated to open in July 2032.

Woolworths is an ASX-listed retailer operating a national supermarket network, including full-line Woolworths supermarkets, Woolworths Metro outlets and online grocery services, together with other retail and consumer businesses.

Fabcot acquires and develops non-residential property for the Woolworths Group’s retail network.

Lakewood Estate Development (No 1) Pty Ltd is a special purpose vehicle created for the purpose of development of the Target Site.
