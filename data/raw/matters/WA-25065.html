Description

Carlisle Health Sydney Pty Ltd proposes to acquire assets owned by Altus Medical Imaging Pty Ltd, Altus Services Pty Ltd, LongReach Australia Pty Ltd, LongReach Support Pty Ltd in its own capacity

Carlisle Health Sydney Pty Ltd proposes to acquire assets owned by Altus Medical Imaging Pty Ltd, Altus Services Pty Ltd, LongReach Australia Pty Ltd, LongReach Support Pty Ltd in its own capacity and as trustee of LongReach Support Services Unit Trust, and LongReach Imaging Pty Ltd, that together comprise the Altus Medical Imaging radiology business (Altus). 

Carlisle Health Sydney Pty Ltd is a wholly owned subsidiary of Carlisle Health Pty Ltd (together, Carlisle Health). Carlisle Health is majority owned by funds managed or advised by private equity firm Quadrant Private Equity.

Carlisle Health is a radiology group with 26 clinics across Queensland and New South Wales. Carlisle Health provides a full range of radiology services to patients, referring practitioners and hospital partners in metropolitan and regional communities.

Altus provides radiology services from a clinic located at 233-235 Victoria Road, Gladesville, New South Wales. Altus provides radiology services to patients, including general X-Ray, CT, MRI, ultrasound, 3D mammography, dental imaging, bone densitometry, and injections and biopsies.
