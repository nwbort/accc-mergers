Description

Xylem Inc.

Xylem Inc. (together with its subsidiaries, including the acquiring entity Xylem Water Solutions U.S.A., Inc., Xylem) proposes to indirectly acquire 100% of the equity interests in Flow Controls Holding, LLC (Flow Controls) (the Acquisition).

Xylem is a water technology company active in the design, manufacturing, and servicing of highly engineered products and solutions across a range of critical applications, including in the water sector. Globally and in Australia, Xylem provides water and wastewater treatment and transport solutions, water analysis and monitoring equipment, pumps (both on a sale and rental basis), and packaged pump systems (including internet of things (IoT) sensors and hardware and associated remote monitoring software for pumps).

Flow Controls comprises four businesses, each of which is involved in the supply of pumps and/or pump-related solutions: Cornell Pump Company, Roper Pump Company, Dynamatic, and High Tide Technologies. Together, these businesses develop, manufacture and supply a range of pumps, flow technologies, and related monitoring and control solutions for a range of industrial applications.
