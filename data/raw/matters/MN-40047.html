Repurpose It Holdings Pty Ltd (Repurpose It) has a 50% ownership stake in Delta Skipz Pty Ltd (Delta Skipz) which operates under the Skipz Recycling brand. Repurpose It proposes to acquire the remaining 50% of the shares in Delta Skipz from its joint venture partner Delta Recycling Pty Ltd (Delta Recycling) (Proposed Transaction). 

Repurpose It operates waste processing facilities at Epping, Montrose and Hallam. At its Epping site, Repurpose It operates large-scale soil washing facility. At its Montrose and Hallam sites, Repurpose It receives and processes timber and mulch which are transformed into garden mulch and soil conditioners. Repurpose It supplies recovered materials to third parties, in competition with traditional suppliers of non-recycled products.

RPI Ballarto Holdings Pty Ltd (a related entity of Repurpose It), owns and operates the solid inert landfill and resource recovery facility at Botanic Ridge, Melbourne, which receives construction and demolition and commercial and industrial waste. 

Skipz Recycling is a skip bin hire and collection business which operates in metropolitan Melbourne, Brisbane and the Gold Coast. Skipz Recycling also operates a transfer station in Port Melbourne.
