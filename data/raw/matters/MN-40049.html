Coles Group Limited (Coles) is an Australian public company (ASX: COL) that primarily operates:

  • supermarkets nationwide through its subsidiary, Coles Supermarkets Australia Pty Ltd (CSA); and

  • retail liquor stores nationwide, trading under the Liquorland, Liquorland Warehouse and Liquorland Cellars brands. 

SpinksCo (Lessor) is a property development company that specialises in retail development projects across Queensland. 

CSA has signed a letter of offer with the lessor, which is intended to facilitate Coles acquiring a leasehold interest in a new convenience-based neighbourhood centre to be developed at 34 Central Avenue, Cannonvale Queensland 4802 for the purposes of a Coles supermarket (the Acquisition).

The proposed Coles supermarket is anticipated to open around March 2030.
