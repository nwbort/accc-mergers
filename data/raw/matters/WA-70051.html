Description

Advent Partners 4 Fund, LP (Advent Partners) proposes to acquire the majority indirect interest in Procare Group Pty Ltd (Procare).

Advent Partners 4 Fund, LP (Advent Partners) proposes to acquire the majority indirect interest in Procare Group Pty Ltd (Procare).

Advent Partners is a Melbourne-based private capital investor that invests in and manages a portfolio of businesses across a range of sectors including healthcare, consumer, education, technology and software. 

Procare is a diversified insurance support services group supplying services to insurers, underwriters, and brokers across Australia. Its key services include loss adjusting, factual and surveillance investigations, injury management, recoveries, forensic accounting and fraud, legal services (via PC Legal Pty Ltd), claims management and consulting, workers’ compensation & investigations training (via a registered training organisation), claims and risk services (via Procare Claims & Risk Pty Ltd), cyber incident response (via Procare Cyber Pty Ltd), and rehabilitation and NDIS services (via PC Ability Pty Ltd). 

AUB Group Limited (AUB), an ASX-listed entity which is comprised of insurance brokers and underwriting agencies, currently owns ~48% of Procare. Management and employees of Procare currently own (directly or indirectly) the balance of Procare. 

On completion of the transaction, existing management and employee shareholders of Procare will retain the remaining minority indirect interest in Procare, and AUB will not hold any interest.
