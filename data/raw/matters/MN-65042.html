Description

Thoma Bravo, UGP LLC. (Thoma Bravo), through its portfolio company Proofpoint, Inc.

Thoma Bravo, UGP LLC. (Thoma Bravo), through its portfolio company Proofpoint, Inc. (Proofpoint), proposes to acquire the entire issued share capital of Varonis Systems, Inc. (Varonis) (the Acquisition).  


Thoma Bravo is a US-based investment firm with a focus on software and technology-enabled services businesses. Thoma Bravo supplies cybersecurity solutions in Australia through its portfolio company Proofpoint. Proofpoint offers an integrated suite of cloud-based solutions to help companies prevent targeted cyber threats and data loss. 


Varonis is a cybersecurity company providing software for identifying, classifying and protecting sensitive data stored across an organisation's on-premises and cloud systems. Varonis’ core product is its data security platform, a unified software platform delivered primarily as a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) product for enterprise environments. Varonis’ data security platform’s principal capability is in 'data security posture management', a security approach that discovers, classifies, and monitors where sensitive data lives across cloud and other environments, to enable risk identification and mitigation.
