OMERS Administration Corporation (OMERS) proposes to acquire an indirect economic interest of approximately 19.6% in AMC AusCo 1 Pty Ltd (La Trobe HoldCo), which is the holding company that controls La Trobe Financial through a limited partnership structure. Brookfield currently owns approximately 71.04% of the La Trobe Holdco.

OMERS is a non-share corporation constituted under the laws of Ontario, Canada and is the administrator of the Ontario Municipal Employees Retirement System Primary Pension Plan (‘OMERS Pension Plan’). OMERS is responsible for pension services, administration and plan valuation as well as making and holding investments. Through its affiliates, OMERS invests globally across a diversified portfolio of asset classes, including public markets, private equity, infrastructure and real estate.

Brookfield Corporation (BN, and together with funds managed and/or advised by BN and/or its affiliates, Brookfield) is a global investment firm that deploys pools of discretionary capital globally for investors across its core businesses, including its asset management business and its operating businesses focused mainly on renewable power and transition, infrastructure, private equity, and real estate.

La Trobe Financial is an alternative asset manager providing investment solutions to Australian investors and borrowers. 

Following completion of the Acquisition, Brookfield will continue to manage the investment in La Trobe HoldCo as lead investor, retaining full operational, capital and exit authority.
