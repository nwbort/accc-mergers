Description

Schlumberger B.V. (Schlumberger), a direct wholly owned subsidiary of SLB N.V.

Schlumberger B.V. (Schlumberger), a direct wholly owned subsidiary of SLB N.V. (SLB), proposes to acquire 100% of the issued share capital, and sole control, of King TopCo Limited , the parent company of the Kelvion group (together with its subsidiaries, Kelvion Group), from King Holdings III, L.P. (the Acquisition). 

SLB is a global technology company listed on the New York Stock Exchange, and together with its subsidiaries, comprises the SLB Group. The SLB Group primarily provides equipment, software and technical services, including subsurface evaluation, drilling and production services, to customers in the energy sector. It operates globally and focuses on decarbonisation, oil and gas, digital services and low-carbon energy technology solutions. SLB Group has recently expanded into the supply of modular data centre infrastructure. 

The Kelvion Group is a global developer and manufacturer of thermal management technologies (that is, heat exchange and cooling solutions, including heat exchangers, evaporators, condensers, coolers and heat exchange coils) and is headquartered in Germany. These products are deployed across four key business segments: 

  1. ‘high tech’ end-markets (for example, data centres); 

  2. ‘conventional’ end-markets such as chemicals, transportation, heavy and light industry, food and beverage, heating, ventilation and air conditioning, and power and energy; 

  3. ‘green tech’ end-markets in the areas of liquefied natural gas, hydrogen renewable energy, heat pumps and carbon capture

  4. repair and maintenance services and/or spare parts. 

In Australia, the Kelvion Group supplies heat exchange and cooling products but does not have any local manufacturing capability.
