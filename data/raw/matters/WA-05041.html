Elevate BidCo Pty Ltd (BidCo) proposes to acquire 100% of the issued shares in Hewison & Associates Pty Ltd (Hewison) from Hewison's existing founding shareholders (the Acquisition). BidCo is a special purpose vehicle and is an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Elevate HoldCo Pty Ltd (HoldCo). HoldCo is currently wholly owned by Pemba Capital Fund III, L.P., a private equity fund managed by Pemba Capital Partners Pty Ltd (Pemba), based in Sydney.

The Acquisition will be implemented by way of a Share Sale Agreement. Following completion, BidCo will exercise control of Hewison.

Pemba is an investor in high-growth and entrepreneurial businesses, with over AUD 2 billion in assets under management. Pemba has interests across various sectors, including business services, education & training, financial services, healthcare, and technology. Relevant to the Acquisition, one of Pemba's portfolio companies is SuperConcepts Pty Ltd (SuperConcepts), which provides self-managed super fund administration services and software to participants in the financial advice and accounting industries. 

Hewison is a Melbourne-based wealth management firm that provides financial advisory, investment strategy and wealth management services to private clients across Australia. Hewison currently provides self-managed super fund administration services to its clients in-house and uses a third-party self-managed super fund software provider for these accounts.
