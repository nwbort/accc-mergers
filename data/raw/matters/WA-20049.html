Bijoma North Property Pty Limited, as trustee for Bijoma North Property Unit Trust (Bijoma Property), and Bijoma North Operating Pty Ltd, as trustee for Bijoma North Operating Unit Trust (Bijoma Operating), propose to acquire the freehold interests and operating hotel businesses of Courthouse Hotel and Kinselas Hotel in Darlinghurst, NSW (the Acquisition). The Acquisition includes freehold land and buildings, hotel business assets, goodwill, equipment and associated liquor licence and gaming entitlements in respect of the Courthouse Hotel and Kinselas Hotel.  

Bijoma Property and Bijoma Operating are entities within the Oscars Group. Oscars Group is an Australian hospitality group with a portfolio of over 40 assets across a variety of segments including pubs and gaming, accommodation, retail liquor, conference and events centres, chartered vessels, and commercial and residential developments. 

The Courthouse Hotel is a pub with boutique accommodation, located at 189 Oxford Street, Darlinghurst, NSW 2010.

Kinselas Hotel is a pub and entertainment venue, located at 383 Bourke Street (Taylor Square), Darlinghurst, NSW 2010.
