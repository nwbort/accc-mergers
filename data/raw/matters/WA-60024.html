Description

Redwood Services Group Australia Pty Ltd (Redwood) proposes to acquire Newtrend I.T.

Redwood Services Group Australia Pty Ltd (Redwood) proposes to acquire Newtrend I.T. Specialists Pty Ltd (Newtrend), through the acquisition of 100% of the shares in its holding company, Kewdale Investments Pty Ltd (the Acquisition). 

Redwood’s subsidiaries supply managed IT services, including the provision of software and hardware, to small-to-medium businesses and enterprises across Australia. Redwood is part of Evergreen Services Group (Evergreen), which acquires managed IT service providers (MSPs) and other technology services companies globally. Evergreen is a portfolio company of Alpine Investors, a private equity firm headquartered in San Francisco, California. 

Newtrend supplies managed IT services, including the provision of software and hardware, to small-to-medium businesses and enterprises.
