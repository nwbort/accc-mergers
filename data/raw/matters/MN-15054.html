Australian Leisure and Hospitality Group Pty Limited (ALH Group), a wholly owned subsidiary of Endeavour Group Limited (Endeavour), proposes to acquire the business assets of a retail liquor store on Russell Island, QLD, the freehold land on which the retail liquor store operates (the Bottleshop Site), and the freehold land for an adjoining commercial lot (the Adjoining Site) from Primeland Holdings Pty Ltd as trustee for the Prime Holdings Unit Trust (Vendor).

The Vendor owns and operates a Thirsty Camel Bottleshop business at the Bottleshop Site. 

Endeavour intends to operate a BWS bottleshop at the Bottleshop Site (but not the Adjoining Site).

The Bottleshop Site is located at 18 High Street, Russell Island QLD 4184. The Adjoining Site is located at 10 – 16 High Street, Russell Island QLD 4184 and is currently leased to a real estate business.

Endeavour’s closest retail liquor stores to the Bottleshop Site are BWS Redland Bay Red Edge, BWS Redland Bay Drive and BWS Redland Bay Village, each on the mainland and located at least 35 minutes away (including a ferry trip). 

Endeavour is an Australian public company (ASX:EDV) that primarily operates: 

  • retail liquor stores nationwide under the BWS and Dan Murphy’s brand 

  • licensed hotel venues under the ALH Hotels banner nationally. These venues are individually branded, offering on-premises food and drinks, accommodation, entertainment and gaming services

  • Pinnacle Drinks, a manufacturer and distributor of liquor products, which it distributes exclusively to the Endeavour retail and hotel network in Australia.
