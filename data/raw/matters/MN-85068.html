Description

Galderma Group AG (Galderma), through its wholly owned subsidiary Galderma Services, Inc, proposes to acquire 100% of ZO SH Holdings, Inc (ZO Skin Health). 

Galderma is a publicly listed company headquartered in Zug, Switzerland. Galderma is active in the research, development, manufacturing, marketing, distribution and sale of skin solutions globally. Galderma operates in three business segments: 

  • Dermatological Skincare, which includes its clinical-grade skincare brand Alastin

  • Injectable Aesthetics

  • Therapeutic Dermatology.  

ZO Skin Health is a skincare company headquartered in Irvine, California. ZO Skin Health supplies clinical-grade skincare products through online, speciality skincare / beauty retailers and professional channels such as authorised medical / clinician practices, beauty-spas and aesthetic clinics, including in Australia. ZO Skin Health is majority owned by funds ultimately controlled by Blackstone Inc.
