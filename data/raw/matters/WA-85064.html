Description

Infosys Singapore Pte. Ltd.

Infosys Singapore Pte. Ltd. (Infosys SG), a wholly owned subsidiary of Infosys Limited (together with its subsidiaries, the Infosys Group), proposes to acquire 75% of the issued share capital in Versent Group Pty Ltd (formerly Telstra Purple Pty Ltd, Telstra Purple) from Telstra Limited (Telstra) (the Acquisition). 

Infosys SG will hold a 75% interest in Telstra Purple and Telstra Limited will retain a 25% interest. 

The Infosys Group is a global provider of next-generation digital services and consulting. In Australia, the Infosys Group has offices in Sydney, Parramatta, Melbourne, Brisbane, Canberra and Perth. It supplies IT and technology solutions to enterprise customers, including IT/cloud solutions, software development and maintenance, outsourcing, business process management, cybersecurity and consulting services. 

Telstra Purple is an Australian provider of cloud, digital, data/AI and IT service management services. Together with its wholly owned subsidiaries, it carries on three businesses: 

  • Versent, which builds and runs cloud-native platforms using its partnerships with Microsoft Azure and Amazon Web Services

  • Telstra Purple Digital, a cloud provider and custom application developer

  • Epicon, an IT service management provider that works predominantly for government clients. 

Infosys Group will also acquire an indirect interest in a passive shareholding of less than 3% in Cloudwave Pty Ltd (trading as NeonNow.AI).
