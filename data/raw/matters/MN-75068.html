Description

The Star Entertainment Group Limited (The Star), Far East Consortium International Limited (FEC) and Chow Tai Fook Enterprises Limited (CTFE

The Star Entertainment Group Limited (The Star), Far East Consortium International Limited (FEC) and Chow Tai Fook Enterprises Limited (CTFE) propose to implement an asset swap through a number of interconnected acquisitions set out below (collectively, the Proposed Transactions). 

The Star currently has:

  • a 33.3% interest in Destination Gold Coast Consortium Pty Ltd as trustee for the DGC Consortium Development Trust, and Destination Gold Coast Consortium Hotel Pty Ltd as trustee for the DGC Consortium Hotel Trust (DGCC Hotel) (together, Destination Gold Coast Consortium). DGCC Hotel is the holding entity of Dorsett Gold Coast Hotel (Dorsett Gold Coast) and Andaz Gold Coast Hotel (Andaz) (expected to open in late 2026), located on the Gold Coast. The remaining two-thirds interest in Destination Gold Coast Consortium is held by FEC and CTFE. 

  • a 50% interest in Festival Car Park Pty Ltd (Festival Car Park), located at 53 Charlotte Street in Brisbane. An approximately 47.6% interest in Festival Car Park is currently held by FEC and CTFE.

  • a 100% interest in the Treasury Hotel and the associated car park located at 130 William Street in Brisbane (together, the Treasury Assets).

The Proposed Transactions involve the following: 

  • The Star will acquire FEC’s and CTFE’s interests in Destination Gold Coast Consortium on the Gold Coast. This will result in The Star’s interest in each of Dorsett Gold Coast and Andaz increasing from 33.3% to full ownership.

  • FEC and CTFE will acquire The Star's interests in the Treasury Assets and Festival Car Park in Brisbane. This will result in FEC and CTFE increasing their aggregate indirect (look-through) interest in Festival Car Park from 47.6% currently, to approximately 95.2%, and acquiring full ownership of the Treasury Assets.
