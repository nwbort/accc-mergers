The State of Queensland (represented by the Department of State Development, Infrastructure and Planning) is the registered proprietor of the land for the proposed Gabba Arena, an indoor arena at Woolloongabba, Brisbane. Gabba Arena is proposed to be Queensland's premier arena for entertainment and sports, and is part of the broader Gabba entertainment and housing precinct project in preparation for the 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games. 

Gather Brisbane (Arena) Pty Ltd (Arena Co) is a special purpose vehicle and a wholly-owned subsidiary of Plenary Origination Pty Ltd, a subsidiary of Plenary Group Holdings Pty Ltd (Plenary). Plenary is an independent long-term investor, developer and manager of public infrastructure, specialising in public-private partnerships and precinct development.

Plenary forms part of the Gather Brisbane Consortium, together with Live Nation Holdings Australasia 2 Pty Ltd, a subsidiary of Live Nation Australasia Pty Ltd (Live Nation), OVG Europe Limited, a subsidiary of Oak View Group, LLC (OVF), and Webuild QLD Pty Ltd, a subsidiary of Webuild S.p.A.

The Gather Brisbane Consortium is one of two shortlisted bidders for the development of the Gabba entertainment and housing precinct.

Arena Co proposes to enter into project documents with the State of Queensland. The project documents cover, among other things:

  • Arena Co's acquisition of a leasehold interest from the State of Queensland for all areas necessary for Arena Co to operate and access Gabba Arena as part of the development of the Gabba entertainment and housing precinct (Ground Lease) (the Acquisition)

  • the State of Queensland's acquisition of a sublease from Arena Co for a right to occupy and use the Gabba Arena

  • the State of Queensland's grant of development rights to Dev Co (Gabba East) (a newly established entity controlled by Plenary), for the development of the Gabba entertainment and housing precinct

  • the State of Queensland's grant of a call option for Arena Co to acquire ownership of Gabba Arena and freehold title in the land at a pre-agreed price

  • a right for the State of Queensland to require Arena Co to acquire ownership of Gabba Arena and freehold title in the land at a pre-agreed price.

In addition to these documents, the State of Queensland will grant an operating licence to Operator Co, a joint venture between Live Nation and OVG, to operate Gabba Arena.

The applicant is specifically seeking a waiver in respect of Arena Co's proposed acquisition of the Ground Lease only.
