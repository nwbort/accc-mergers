Description

Intega Canada Holdings Ltd., a wholly owned subsidiary of Kiwa N.V.

Intega Canada Holdings Ltd., a wholly owned subsidiary of Kiwa N.V. (Kiwa) and SHV Holdings, proposes to acquire 100% of the shares in CSA Group Testing & Certification Inc.

Kiwa is a global testing, inspection and certification (TIC) company for clients across a broad range of industries. It is wholly owned by SHV Holdings, a Dutch conglomerate.

In Australia, Kiwa has four business lines, providing testing for mining, civil construction and infrastructure services, construction materials testing, geotechnical services consulting and ISO certification.

CSA Group is a global TIC company based in Canada. It has a limited presence in Australia, in factory certification.
