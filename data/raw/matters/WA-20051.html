Tetra Tech Australia Group Holdings Pty Ltd (Tetra Tech) proposes to acquire 100% of the shares in Pitt & Sherry (Operations) Pty Ltd and its subsidiaries (Pitt & Sherry) through a Share Sale and Purchase Agreement (the Acquisition).

Tetra Tech is a wholly owned subsidiary of Tetra Tech, Inc., a global public corporation listed on the NASDAQ, and the holding company for various subsidiaries. It is headquartered in Pasadena, California. Tetra Tech’s subsidiaries are involved in a variety of industries, in particular, water, energy, infrastructure, mining and resources, defence and development, and provide high-end consulting, technical and advisory services in Australia to a variety of customers, both public and private. Tetra Tech also has operations and interests across the Asia-Pacific region.

Pitt & Sherry is an Australian-based consultancy firm offering engineering, environmental and building surveying services Australia-wide, with a strong presence in Tasmania.
