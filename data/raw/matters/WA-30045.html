On 15 October 2026, PE Lumen Holding IV Limited (PE Lumen), a company owned and controlled by investment entities that are directly or indirectly managed and/or advised (including through intermediaries and funds) by Platinum Equity Advisors, LLC and ultimately controlled by Platinum Equity, LLC (Platinum Equity Group), entered into a sale and purchase agreement with the shareholders of Light Holding S.p.A. (Light Holding and, together with its subsidiary, Trime S.r.l. (Trime)). At completion, a new Italian company to be incorporated and controlled by PE Lumen will acquire 100% of the issued share capital and voting rights in Light Holding.

Platinum Equity Group is a US-headquartered global private equity and investment strategies firm. It acquires and operates companies in a broad range of sectors including, for example, business services, information technology, telecommunications, logistics, metal services, manufacturing and distribution.

Platinum Equity Group’s portfolio includes Rehlko, which manufactures and supplies mobile generators (or gensets) and diesel engines.

Trime, a company headquartered in Italy, designs and manufactures professional machines for construction sites and rental companies worldwide. Its product portfolio includes lighting towers, gensets, security towers, hydraulic tools, as well as spare parts and services. In Australia, Trime's activities are conducted through its majority interest in PR Power, an Australian distributor of lighting towers and gensets.
