Samsung Biologics proposes to make an all-cash public tender offer to acquire all publicly held registered shares of the PolyPeptide Group AG (the Target). Under the Transaction Agreement, the Target will recommend the tender offer for acceptance by the holders of the Target’s shares (the Acquisition).

Draupnir Holding B.V. (the largest individual shareholder of the Target) also signed a Tender Undertaking with Samsung Biologics pursuant to which it agreed to tender all its shares corresponding to approximately 55.65% of the Target’s total outstanding shares (excluding treasury shares). The all-cash public tender offer is worth approximately CHF 1.46 billion (~AUD 2.59 billion).

Samsung Biologics, headquartered in South Korea, is a contract development and manufacturing organisation (CDMO) which offers services that range from research and development to commercial manufacturing. Samsung Biologics provides CDMO services for active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) and finished drug products, as well as contract research organisation services. 

Samsung Biologics is listed on the Korean stock exchange and is currently owned by Samsung C&T Corporation (43.06%), Samsung Electronics Co. Ltd. (31.22%) and others (25.72%). 

The Target is headquartered in Switzerland, is a specialised global CDMO for peptide-based APIs using chemically synthesised production methods. It services primarily pharma and biotech customers by offering products and services from pre-clinical to commercial stages. The Target also produces a range of generic peptides.
