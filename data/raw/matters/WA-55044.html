Description

Foresight Kinetic CV UK HoldCo Limited (the Buyer) will:

Foresight Kinetic CV UK HoldCo Limited (the Buyer) will:

  • acquire Infrastructure Capital Services Pty Limited as trustee for the ICG Kinetic Trust’s (the Seller’s) entire shareholding (of approximately 24.4%) in Kinetic UK Holdco, Limited (the Target), and

  • as the Seller’s nominee, subscribe for additional shares to restore the Seller’s proportionate holding to around 29.9% following an earlier equity subscription by TPG Lux 2025 SC XI, S.à r.l. (TPG)

(the Acquisition). 

The Buyer is an investment holding vehicle for Foresight Kinetic CV SCSp's (CV’s) investment in the Target. CV is a Luxembourg special limited partnership established as a continuation fund. 

Foresight Group Holdings Limited (Foresight Group) is a London Stock Exchange-listed investment manager. It invests in renewable energy, battery storage, grid infrastructure, natural capital, and social, transport and digital infrastructure. The Seller and Buyer are and will remain ultimately controlled by Foresight Group. 

The Target is the holding company of the Kinetic group and the Go-Ahead Group, which provide bus and rail passenger transport services in Australia, New Zealand, the UK, Ireland, Norway, Sweden and Singapore. Its rail operations are conducted outside Australia through Go-Ahead. The Target is incorporated in England and Wales. 

TPG currently holds approximately 75.5% of the shares in the Target and controls the Target. Post-Acquisition, TPG will hold approximately 70.1% of the shares in the Target and will continue to control the Target. 

Foresight Group will retain exclusive management and control of CV through Foresight Kinetic GP S.à r.l.
