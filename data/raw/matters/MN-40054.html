Herron Todd White (Bidco) Pty Ltd (HTW Bidco) proposes to acquire 100% of the issued share capital in Valuation Services (Qld) Pty Ltd (VSQ) from 4 individual directors / shareholders of VSQ. 

Herron Todd White (Holdings) Pty Ltd (HTW Holdings) is the ultimate parent company of HTW Bidco. HTW Holdings and its connected entities (collectively, the HTW Group) provide property valuation and related property advisory services throughout Australia. HTW Group’s core service offering comprises residential, commercial and agribusiness property valuation services, supplied principally to Australian authorised deposit-taking institutions such as the major banks and other financial institutions, as well as to government agencies, property developers, legal practitioners and private clients. HTW Group is majority-owned by funds managed by QPE No. 7 Management Pty Ltd, an entity within the Quadrant Private Equity group.

VSQ is a privately owned valuation practice that provides commercial, institutional and specialist property valuation services throughout Queensland. 

VSQ traded as ‘Knight Frank Valuation & Advisory Queensland’ and ‘Knight Frank Health and Aged Care Queensland’ from 2019 to June 2026.

VSQ currently operates under a licence from HTW Group and trades as Herron Todd White Institutional, resides in HTW Group’s Brisbane offices and operates on HTW Group’s network and systems.
