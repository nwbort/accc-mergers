Description

Waterfront Logistics HoldCo Pty Ltd (Waterfront Logistics) proposes to acquire 100% of the issued shares in Monson Logistics Pty Ltd, Monson Shipping Pty Ltd and Monquip

Waterfront Logistics HoldCo Pty Ltd (Waterfront Logistics) proposes to acquire 100% of the issued shares in Monson Logistics Pty Ltd, Monson Shipping Pty Ltd and Monquip Pty Ltd (together, Monson Logistics) from Bruton Investments Pty Ltd, Bruce William Monson and Toni Anne Monson (the Acquisition). 

Waterfront Logistics is owned by First Sentier Investors (Australia) RE Ltd as trustee of the Australia New Zealand Infrastructure Fund (trading as Igneo Infrastructure Partners). Igneo Infrastructure Partners is the infrastructure asset management business of the First Sentier Group, which is ultimately owned by Mitsubishi UFJ Financial Group. 

Waterfront Logistics, through its subsidiaries, operates the Strait Link business (Strait Link), which provides domestic shipping services across Bass Strait between the Port of Melbourne and the Port of Burnie, carrying containerised and roll-on/roll-off cargo. Strait Link also provides land logistics services in Tasmania and at the Port of Melbourne, including general and refrigerated freight, warehousing, rail operations and dangerous goods services. 

Monson Logistics is a Tasmanian-based provider of freight transport and logistics services. Its services include road and rail transport, warehousing, Australian Border Force (ABF) and the Department of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries (DAFF) bond facilities, empty international container parks and Tasmanian Freight Equalisation Scheme (TFES) claims services.
