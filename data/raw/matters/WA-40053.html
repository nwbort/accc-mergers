Description

Emera Incorporated (Emera) proposes to acquire all of the outstanding Class II voting and Class I non-voting shares of ATCO Ltd.

Emera Incorporated (Emera) proposes to acquire all of the outstanding Class II voting and Class I non-voting shares of ATCO Ltd. (ATCO) and the Class B common and Class A non-voting shares of Canadian Utilities Limited (Canadian Utilities) not already held by ATCO, in exchange for Emera shares, by way of a court-approved plan of arrangement under section 192 of the Canada Business Corporations Act (the Acquisition).

Before completion, ATCO’s businesses other than Canadian Utilities will be transferred to a new corporation, SpinCo, and SpinCo shares will be distributed to current ATCO shareholders. SpinCo will not be acquired by Emera as part of the Acquisition.

Emera is a provider of energy services headquartered in Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada, owning and operating a portfolio of electric and natural gas utilities serving approximately 2.1 million customers, with its largest operations in Florida, U.S.A. and additional operations in Atlantic Canada and the Caribbean. Emera is listed on the Toronto and New York Stock Exchanges and has no Australian operations.

Canadian Utilities and ATCO own and operate Western Australia’s largest gas distribution network through its Australian subsidiaries, including the Mid-West and South-West Gas Distribution Systems (Perth metropolitan area, Geraldton, Bunbury, Busselton and regional hubs), the Kalgoorlie system and the Albany LPG network. ATCO also owns and operates two power plants in Australia, being the 86MW gas fired Karratha Power Station in the Pilbara and the 180MW Osborne Cogeneration Power Station in South Australia, which is owned by an incorporated joint venture in which ATCO holds 50% with Origin Energy.
