Apiam Animal Health Pty Ltd (Apiam) proposes to acquire 100% of the issued share capital in Caroline Springs Veterinary Centre & Animal Hospital Pty Ltd (CS Veterinary Centre) (the Acquisition). CS Veterinary Centre owns and operates the business known as Caroline Springs Veterinary Centre and Animal Hospital. 

Apiam is a rural veterinary businesses, comprising more than 80 veterinary clinics and ancillary business sites across Australia, and supplies a range of veterinary services across Australia, including companion animal, equine, dairy, beef cattle, sheep and goat, pig, poultry and mixed animal veterinary services, together with veterinary diagnostics, emergency and after-hours services, reproductive and herd health services, and pathology, laboratory and advisory services. Apiam is ultimately owned and controlled by funds managed and advised by Adamantem Capital Management. 

CS Veterinary Centre is a full-service companion animal veterinary practice located in Caroline Springs, Victoria. The practice provides general veterinary care, pathology services and surgical procedures.
