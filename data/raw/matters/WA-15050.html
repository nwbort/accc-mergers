Description

NDC HoldCo Pty Ltd (together with its subsidiaries, National Dental Care), through its wholly-owned subsidiary, NDC Australia Pty Limited, proposes to acquire 100% of the business

NDC HoldCo Pty Ltd (together with its subsidiaries, National Dental Care), through its wholly-owned subsidiary, NDC Australia Pty Limited, proposes to acquire 100% of the business assets comprising the Dentists of Tasmania clinic from The Trustee for Dentists of Tasmania Trust (the Acquisition).

National Dental Care is a dental services organisation that operates a network of dental clinics that provide a range of general dentistry services. National Dental Care has dental clinics in all Australian states and territories, including one clinic in Tasmania. National Dental Care is majority owned by funds managed by Crescent Capital Partners, an Australian private equity firm.

Dentists of Tasmania is a dental clinic located in Sorell, Tasmania, that provides a range of general dentistry services.
