Description

MAHF Custodian Pty Ltd, as custodian for RGM TT Pty Ltd (as trustee for the Hotel Canobolas Trust) and Redcape Hotel Group Pty Ltd (together, the Acquirers) propose to acquire

MAHF Custodian Pty Ltd, as custodian for RGM TT Pty Ltd (as trustee for the Hotel Canobolas Trust) and Redcape Hotel Group Pty Ltd (together, the Acquirers) propose to acquire the:

  • hotel business intellectual property rights

  • goodwill

  • hotelier’s licence

  • gaming machine entitlements under that licence

  • a lease of the hotel premises, and 

  • the freehold land interest.

of Hotel Canobolas, located in NSW (the Target) from Canobolas Property Pty Limited and Canobolas Pub Pty Limited (the Acquisition).

The Target is a hotel offering accommodation, food, beverage, gaming, entertainment and function / event services, located at 248‑266 Summer Street, Orange NSW 2800. It has 69 accommodation rooms with 45 rooms in operation, and 17 gaming machine entitlements under its hotelier’s licence.

The Acquirers are part of MA Financial’s Redcape Fund, which owns and operates community pubs and hotels. The Redcape Fund portfolio currently has 42 pubs and hotels in NSW, Queensland and Victoria.
