Waveconn Infrastructure Pty Ltd as trustee of the Waveconn Infrastructure Trust (Waveconn) proposes to acquire 42 Optus sites (designated as Deferred Sites) from Optus Mobile Pty Ltd (Optus) under a Tower Asset Transfer Agreement. The Tower Asset Transfer Agreement is a broader agreement that was entered into in September 2025. 

The Waveconn Group is a provider of neutral-host telecommunications infrastructure in Australia, supplying bespoke and standard telecommunication structures and associated infrastructure for co-location. Waveconn currently owns and operates a passive neutral-host digital infrastructure portfolio of towers, building rooftops and wireless infrastructure assets across Australia. 

Waveconn was formed through OMERS’ acquisition of the TPG/Vodafone tower and rooftop portfolio and Stilmark. Waveconn operates independently, with no direct or indirect mobile network operator ownership or funding. 

The Deferred Sites are mainly mobile coverage sites co-funded by Government. Their sale forms part of Optus’ broader strategy to focus on service provision rather than passive infrastructure ownership. The Deferred Sites comprise the site tenure, tower or rooftop structures and associated passive infrastructure.
