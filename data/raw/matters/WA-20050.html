Aware Super Pty Limited as trustee of Aware Super (Aware Super) through its subsidiary AS Infrastructure Investments UK Ltd, proposes to subscribe for a non-controlling interest in Foresight Kinetic CV SCSp (Foresight Kinetic CV), an investment continuation vehicle, which is managed on a discretionary basis by Foresight Group LLP and its affiliates (Foresight Group), and which will hold an indirect underlying interest in the Kinetic Group (Kinetic).

Aware Super is an Australian profit-for-members superannuation fund with approximately 1.3 million members and approximately $245 billion in funds under management. 

Foresight Group is an investment manager in renewable energy, battery storage, grid infrastructure, natural capital, and social, transport and digital infrastructure, operating across the United Kingdom, Europe and Australia. 

Kinetic is a provider of bus and rail passenger transport services across Australia, New Zealand, the UK, Ireland, Norway, Sweden and Singapore.
