Protech Group (Aust) Pty Ltd (Protech) proposes to acquire 100% of the shares of ActivateRail Pty Ltd ACN 617 749 809 (ActivateRail). 

Protech is an Australian based workforce provider to technical industries including renewables, building and construction, civil construction and infrastructure, industrial maintenance and engineering, mining and resources, traffic management, rail operations, asphalt and road surfacing, warehousing and manufacturing and defence. 

ActivateRail supplies rail safety and site management and supervision services to utilities, engineering and professional service organisations.
