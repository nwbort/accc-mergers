Description

General Pharma Holdings Pty Ltd (General Pharma), through its wholly-owned subsidiary Salisbury HoldCo Pty Ltd, proposes to acquire 100% of the shares in Mayne Pharma International

General Pharma Holdings Pty Ltd (General Pharma), through its wholly-owned subsidiary Salisbury HoldCo Pty Ltd, proposes to acquire 100% of the shares in Mayne Pharma International Pty Ltd (Mayne Pharma) from Mayne Pharma Group Limited, an ASX-listed pharmaceutical company. 

Mayne Pharma develops, manufactures, markets and sells branded and generic prescription medicines and over-the-counter (OTC) remedies. It supplies a portfolio of 20 branded and generic prescription medicines and OTC remedies to pharmacies and hospitals in Australia. Mayne also provides contract manufacturing services for branded and generic medicines and OTC remedies from its manufacturing facility in Salisbury, South Australia.

Orion Laboratories Pty Ltd (trading as Evaris Pharma) (Evaris) is a subsidiary of General Pharma that develops, manufactures, markets and supplies generic and branded off-patent prescription medicines, OTC remedies and healthcare products in Australia and New Zealand. Evaris also provides contract manufacturing services for medicines, OTC remedies and healthcare products from its manufacturing facility in Balcatta, Western Australia. 

General Pharma is controlled by Genesis Capital (Genesis), an Australian private equity firm that invests in businesses operating in the healthcare sector.
