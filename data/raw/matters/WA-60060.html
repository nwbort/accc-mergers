A number of fund entities advised and/or managed by affiliates of Hamilton Lane Advisors, LLC (Hamilton Lane) propose to acquire interests of up to 31.1% in Foresight Kinetic CV SCSp (the CV). 

Hamilton Lane is a global investment management and advisory company based in the US. Hamilton Lane is the Lead Investor Representative for the entities proposed to be admitted as limited partners of the CV. The entities acquiring interests in the CV (as limited partners) comprise HL Global Private Infra Fund Master SCSp, HL Billy Impact PE Master Fund LP, Hamilton Lane Infrastructure Opportunities Fund II Holdings-2 LP, HL PIF Splitter LLC, HL-WHPL Co-Invest, LP and SREH HL PE 1 (Master) LP (collectively, the HL Investors), and are all entities advised and/or managed by Hamilton Lane. 

The CV is a newly formed special limited partnership established as a continuation fund for the purposes of acquiring an interest in Kinetic UK HoldCo Limited (Kinetic HoldCo). Kinetic HoldCo is the ultimate holding company of the Kinetic Group, which supplies bus and rail passenger transport services in Australia, New Zealand, the UK, Ireland, Norway, Sweden and Singapore. Its rail operations are conducted outside Australia through its subsidiary Go-Ahead. 

TPG Lux 2025 SC XI, S.à r.l. (a fund managed by TPG Inc.) currently holds ~75.5% of the shares in and controls Kinetic HoldCo, with the remaining ~24.4% held by Infrastructure Capital Services Pty Limited as trustee for the ICG Kinetic Trust (ICG Kinetic Trust). 

Foresight Kinetic CV UK HoldCo Limited (CV HoldCo) as the investment holding vehicle for the CV will acquire ICG Kinetic Trust’s entire shareholding in Kinetic HoldCo and subscribe for additional shares to hold ~29.9% of Kinetic HoldCo following the transaction. 

Both CV HoldCo and ICG Kinetic Trust are ultimately controlled, and will continue to be controlled following the Acquisition, by Foresight Group Holdings Limited (Foresight Group). Foresight Group is a London Stock Exchange listed investment manager. Foresight Group will retain exclusive management and control of the CV through Foresight Kinetic GP S.à r.l.

Post-Acquisition, TPG will hold approximately 70.1% of the shares in the Target and will continue to control the Target. Hamilton Lane and the HL Investors will have a small, non-controlling interest in the Target.
