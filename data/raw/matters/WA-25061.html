Description

Volaris Group Inc (Volaris) is a Canadian-headquartered company that acquires and manages software businesses globally.

Volaris Group Inc (Volaris) is a Canadian-headquartered company that acquires and manages software businesses globally. It is the parent entity of the Volaris Operating Group and a wholly owned subsidiary of Constellation Software Inc. (CSI). The CSI group of companies acquire, manage, and build industry-specific software businesses globally in a variety of public and private sector industries. Volaris Group Australia Pty Ltd, a wholly owned subsidiary of Volaris, will establish a new 100% owned subsidiary to undertake the acquisition (Volaris NewCo).

Volaris has 20 companies within its Australian portfolio, focused on marketing technology (including Delacon, Pegasus, Decideware, AudioNET and Broadcast MAP) and education technology (including TASS and Edumate, which provide school administration and student management systems to Australian schools).

Spinifex IT Group Holdings Pty Ltd (SpinifexIT) is an Australian-founded software company that develops and supplies Systems, Applications and Products in Data Processing (SAP) payroll automation tools. Its principal product group (referred to as Easy Suite) comprises of SAP certified solutions that automate payroll validation, reconciliation, reporting and data migration processes for organisations using SAP Human Capital Management (HCM) and SuccessFactors systems.

SpinifexIT was founded in Melbourne in 2003 and operates across 45 countries, serving over 450 clients, including enterprise and government customers. It also offers a separate, early-stage cloud-based document generation and management product called Strato, which operates independently of the Easy Suite business.

Volaris NewCo proposes to acquire 100% of the fully diluted shares of the core business of SpinifexIT (the Acquisition). The core business of SpinifexIT is Easy Suite. The Acquisition excludes SpinifexIT’s Strato business division, which will be divested by the shareholders prior to closing.
