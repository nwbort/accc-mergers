Evans Bidco Pty Ltd (Evans Bidco), an entity 50% indirectly owned by funds managed by Crescent Capital Partners Management Pty Limited (Crescent Capital), 49% indirectly owned by Timothy Vorbach and 1% indirectly owned by Jocelyn Jacomina Rosales and Mateo Tarun Roseles, proposes to acquire Humanverse Pty Ltd, the ultimate holding company of TOA Global Pty Ltd (TOA Global) (the Acquisition). 

Evans Bidco holds 100% of the shares in Emapta Hold Co Pte. Ltd. (Emapta). Emapta is a Singapore-registered provider of outsourcing services (including workforce migration, Employer of Record services, and workforce transformation advisory services) to customers across a range of industries, such as finance and accounting, retail, private equity, IT, real estate, logistics, healthcare, and telecommunications and energy. 

Crescent Capital is an alternative asset manager covering Australian and New Zealand private equity, private credit and active listed equities. 

TOA Global is located on the Gold Coast in Queensland, Australia, and provides outsourced staffing solutions globally, connecting individuals in the Philippines with international companies to meet their staffing requirements. TOA Global operates across Australia, the USA, Canada, Philippines, South Africa, and India.
