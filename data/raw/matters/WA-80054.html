Equans Horizon Topco Pty Limited (trading as ‘Equans’) (Equans ANZ), proposes to acquire the majority of the issued share capital in Credible Audio Visual Solutions Pty Ltd (CAVS) through a share purchase agreement (the Acquisition). 

In Australia, Equans ANZ is headquartered in Sydney and supplies a broad range of technical services, including electrical (including energy renewable projects and grid connections) and communications installation, facility management services such as the installation and maintenance of heating, ventilation and air conditioning and fire protection products, digital and static signage services and audio-visual (AV) integration. Its AV services are provided to government, commercial and industrial customers across Australia. These services are typically delivered as part of larger integrated projects (such as infrastructure upgrades or building services), rather than on a standalone basis. 

CAVS is a NSW-based AV design and integration company with one location in Western Sydney. It supplies AV and video conferencing technology services for commercial, government and public sector clients across Australia, primarily in NSW. Its principal activities comprise end-to-end AV services including system design, equipment supply, installation and integration, typically on a standalone basis or as part of technology focused projects.
