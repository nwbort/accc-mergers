VetPartners Australia Pty Ltd (VetPartners), through a newly incorporated holding company, Unity HoldCo Pty Ltd, proposes to acquire 100% of the issued share capital in VetLove Holdings Pty Ltd, the parent entity of the VetLove group which holds majority shareholdings in the group’s 15 veterinary clinics (together, VetLove) (the Acquisition).

VetPartners supplies general veterinary services to companion animals via a network of 202 clinics across Australia, including 51 clinics in Queensland and 50 in New South Wales. VetPartners also operates a range of businesses in the broader animal health sector including the Brisbane Veterinary Specialist Centre and other specialist and emergency hospitals, United Vets Group which is a veterinary buying group, Pet Chemist which is an online veterinary pharmacy and Pet Angel which is a pet crematorium. EQT Fund Management S.à r.l., which indirectly holds and solely controls VetPartners, also holds an interest in Dechra Pharmaceuticals, a global veterinary pharmaceuticals supplier.

VetLove operates 15 general veterinary services clinics across South East Queensland and Northern New South Wales.
