Description

Journey Personal Care France SAS, a subsidiary of Journey Personal Care Holdings Ltd.

Journey Personal Care France SAS, a subsidiary of Journey Personal Care Holdings Ltd. (Attindas), proposes to acquire 100% of the share capital of Financière de la Salicorne S.A.S., the holding company of the Activ Medical Disposable group (and its subsidiaries) (AMD) pursuant to a Securities Transfer Agreement.

Attindas designs, manufactures and markets absorbent hygiene products globally and in Australia, including adult incontinence products. Its products are sold under brands including Attends, Indas and Comfees, as well as under private-label brands for retailers. 

Attindas is under the sole control of certain funds managed by American Industrial Partners (AIP). AIP is a private equity firm based in New York, US, with a focus on industrial businesses. 

AIP controls Global Cellulose Fibers, a producer and supplier of cellulose fibres, including fluff pulp, a raw material used in the manufacture of absorbent hygiene products, such as adult incontinence products. Global Cellulose Fibers operates production facilities in the U.S., Canada and Poland, and supplies its products globally. 

AMD is a designer, manufacturer and distributor of absorbent hygiene and disposable medical products, with a particular focus on adult incontinence products including minor volumes in Australia.
