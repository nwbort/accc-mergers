Endeavour Group Limited (Endeavour) proposes to acquire a leasehold interest for retail space at 137 Thompson Avenue, Cowes VIC 3922 (the Proposed Site) from 137 Thompson Ave Cowes Pty Ltd (the Landlord) (the Acquisition). 

Endeavour proposes to operate a Dan Murphy's on the Proposed Site, with a trading area of approximately 703 square metres. 

The closest Endeavour sites to the proposed Dan Murphy’s are BWS Cowes and BWS Phillip Island.

Endeavour is an Australian public company that primarily operates: 

  • retail liquor stores nationwide under the BWS and Dan Murphy’s brands 

  • licensed hotel venues under the ALH Hotels banner in all state and territories. These venues are individually branded venues offering on-premises food and drinks, entertainment, gaming services and, in some cases, accommodation

  • Pinnacle Drinks, a manufacturer and distributor of liquor products, which it distributes exclusively to the Endeavour retail and hotel network in Australia. 

The Landlord is an Australian privately-owned company established to hold the land at 137 Thompson Avenue, Cowes VIC 3922. The Landlord is owned by Holiday Lifestyle Villages Pty Ltd. The Landlord and its connected entities do not supply wholesale or retail takeaway packaged liquor in Australia.
