Coles Group Limited (Coles) is an Australian public company (ASX: COL) that primarily operates: 

  • supermarkets nationwide through its subsidiary, Coles Supermarkets Australia Pty Ltd (CSA); and 

  • retail liquor stores nationwide, trading under the Liquorland, Liquorland Warehouse and Liquorland Cellars brands. 

Coles also has a property development arm, Coles Group Property Developments Ltd (CGPD), that buys land and constructs shopping centres, standalone supermarkets and / or liquor outlets and then sells these completed sites to third party buyers. 

JD 156 Jonson Street Pty Ltd (Lessor) is an Australian private mixed-use development company focused on developing retail and residential developments in the Northern Rivers region in New South Wales. 

CSA has signed a letter of offer (LOO) with the Lessor, which is intended to facilitate Coles acquiring a leasehold interest in a single lot within a new mixed-use development at 148-158 Jonson Street, Byron Bay NSW 2481 for the purposes of a Coles supermarket (the Acquisition). 

The mixed-use centre will feature Coles as an anchor supermarket, specialty retail, wellness and residential apartments.
