Total Risk Management Pty Limited (TRM), as trustee of the Russell Investments Pooled Superannuation Trust (PST), proposes to subscribe for units in the Flag Index Plus Fund V (Flag Fund), a ‘fund of one’ unit trust established principally for PST’s investment (the Acquisition). TRM may subscribe in one or more instalments. After the first subscription, the PST will hold all of the units in the Flag Fund.

TRM and Russell Investment Management Limited (RIML) are part of the Russell Investments Group (Russell Investments), a global investment firm. In Australia, Russell Investments provides superannuation (through the Russell Investments Master Trust (Master Trust)), multi-manager investment products, implemented consulting and related services. Russell Investments frequently selects external investment managers and strategies for its portfolios. The PST is a pooled superannuation trust whose units are held by the Master Trust, of which TRM is also trustee. RIML is the investment manager of the PST and the Master Trust, and the PST invests mainly through managed funds operated by RIML.

The Flag Fund is an unregistered Australian unit trust. Equity Trustees Limited (Equity Trustees) is the trustee of the Flag Fund and its manager is Flag Asset Management Pty Ltd (Flag). Flag is an Australian investment manager that designs and manages fixed income investment strategies for institutional investors.

The Flag Fund will hold notes issued by Flag Income Notes 3 Pty Ltd (Flag Notes), a wholly owned subsidiary of Flag, and swaps linked to the MSCI All Country World Index ex Australia, together with cash. It aims to deliver the return of that index plus a margin. The Flag Fund is an investment vehicle, and its only activity will be holding these investments for the PST. Units in the Flag Fund will be solely held by TRM as trustee for the PST. Other investors may invest in Flag Notes via separate vehicles.
