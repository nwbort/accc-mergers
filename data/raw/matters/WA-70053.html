AVC Operations Pty Limited (AVC) proposes to acquire substantially all of the assets of the licensed hotel business trading as Central Hotel in Beaconsfield, Victoria (Central Hotel), including the exclusive right to carry on the business in succession of the vendor, and the benefit of the lease, from Beaconsfield Tavern (Operations) Pty Ltd as trustee for Oliver Hume Beaconsfield Tavern Operations Trust  (Vendor), under an Asset Sale Agreement (the Acquisition).

AVC is a subsidiary of Australian Venue Co. Holdings Limited which operates over 200 licensed venues throughout Australia and New Zealand, primarily pubs, as well as some bars, clubs and licensed restaurants, and hotel/motels with accommodation services. AVC’s venues are individually branded and are mostly located in inner-city and metropolitan areas, offering a range of products and services including food, alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages, function rooms, live entertainment and gaming facilities.

Central Hotel is a licensed hotel and associated bottle shop located at 1 Old Princes Highway, Beaconsfield VIC 3807. It is a typical Australian pub/hotel with a bistro space, sports bar, and alfresco dining space.
