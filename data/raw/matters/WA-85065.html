DWML Subsidiary Pty Ltd as trustee for the DREP2 LDK JV Trust (DREP2) has entered into a Unit Sale Deed to acquire 50% of the units in the LDK Healthcare Unit Trust (LDK Healthcare) from Anglican Community Services Investment Holdings Pty Ltd (Anglicare) (the Acquisition).

Currently, 50% of the units in LDK Healthcare are held by Aspire LDK Pty Ltd (Aspire) as trustee for the Aspire LDK Unit Trust and 50% of the units are held by Anglicare. Following the Proposed Acquisition, Aspire and DREP2 will each hold 50% of the units in LDK Healthcare.

LDK Healthcare is a retirement and aged care provider in Australia. It operates three retirement villages:

  1. The Landings at North Turramurra, NSW

  2. Greenway Views at Tuggeranong, ACT

  3. Amberfield at Weston Creek, ACT.

A fourth retirement village, The Baybrook at Brighton, QLD, is under development.

DREP2 is a trust managed by Dexus Real Estate Partnership 2 (DREP2 Fund), a fund managed by Dexus Group (Dexus).

Dexus (or Dexus affiliated entities) own ~64% of the units in DREP2. Golden Active Co-Investment Trust (Golden Active) owns ~36% of the units in DREP2. Golden Active is a Dexus managed trust with Dexus Wholesale Management Ltd as trustee. The sole unitholder of Golden Active is the National Pension Service of the Republic of Korea (NPS), a South Korean public pension fund.

Dexus owns and manages office property, industrial properties, shopping centres and healthcare properties in Australia. Dexus does not operate any retirement village accommodation in Australia. For completeness, Dexus (through the DREP2 Fund) has an equity interest in ‘The Merewether’, a luxury retirement community being constructed in Newcastle, NSW, which is expected to complete in mid to late 2027. The Merewether is being developed and managed by a third party (Third.i).

NPS is a state-run public pension fund based in Seoul, South Korea. NPS provides financial support and retirement benefits for Korean citizens. An NPS-owned fund owns the retirement villages operated by the Aveo Group in QLD, NSW, VIC and Tasmania.
