Ampol Energy Pty Ltd, a wholly owned subsidiary of Ampol Limited, (Ampol) proposes to acquire 100% of the shares in Fast Cities Australia Pty Ltd trading as Evie Networks (Evie Networks) (the Acquisition). 

Ampol is a transport energy provider which engages in purchasing, refining, and distributing petroleum products in Australia and has a network of fuel and retail convenience sites. Ampol also has operations and interests across Singapore, New Zealand, Philippines and the United States of America. In addition, Ampol has an energy solutions business focused on electric vehicle charging and renewable fuels. 

Ampol has an electric vehicle (EV) charging network which operates under the AmpCharge brand with approximately 400 charging bays across 104 sites in all states and territories other than South Australia, Tasmania and the Northern Territory.   

Evie Networks is an EV charging infrastructure company with an EV charging network operating under the Evie brand with approximately 1,000 charging bays across 323 sites in all states and territories in Australia. Evie also operates 33 managed sites. Shares in Evie Networks are held by: 

  • Fast Cities Nominees Pty Ltd as trustee for the bare trust in favour of LFSP Beneficiaries

  • St Baker Fund II Co-Invest Pty Ltd as trustee for the St Baker Fund II Co-Investment Trust 

  • St Baker Energy Holdings Pty Ltd as trustee for the St Baker Energy Innovation Trust

  • One Managed Investment Funds Limited as custodian for One Fund Services Ltd as trustee for the StB Capital Partners Energy Transition Fund II, and 

  • RACQ Operations Pty Ltd.
