Description

Accenture Venture Holdings, LLC, an indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of Accenture plc (together with its direct and indirect subsidiaries, Accenture), proposes to acquire

Accenture Venture Holdings, LLC, an indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of Accenture plc (together with its direct and indirect subsidiaries, Accenture), proposes to acquire 100% of the shares in runZero, Inc. (runZero). 

Accenture is a global professional services company headquartered in Dublin, Ireland. It provides a broad range of services across strategy, consulting, digital, technology, and operations designed to assist public and private clients. 

Accenture provides a range of services in the cybersecurity space from strategic planning through managed security services, operating as both a strategic consultant and systems integrator, working with technology solution partners to offer full-service offerings to customers. 

runZero is an asset discovery technology supplier, and supplies Operational Technology (OT) cybersecurity software globally (including in Australia). Asset discovery technology allows users to identify, map and track operational assets (e.g., industrial devices, systems, and networks), including status, configuration and vulnerabilities at site level and in real time. runZero is headquartered in the US. 

Accenture has separately notified its proposed acquisition of Dragos, Inc (MN-50032), which the ACCC is also assessing.
