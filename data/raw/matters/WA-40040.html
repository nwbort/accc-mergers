Criterion Rocky Op Co Pty Ltd and Waymark Hotels Properties No 46 Pty Ltd, which are part of the Waymark Hotels Group, propose to acquire the land and business of the Criterion Hotel Motel in Rockhampton, Queensland (the Acquisition).

The Waymark Hotels Group owns and operates hotels (pubs) that provide food, drink (including alcohol), gaming machines, accommodation services, and takeaway liquor retailing (bottle shops). It currently operates pubs and bottle shops in regional Queensland and the Northern Territory.

The Criterion Hotel Motel is a commercial hotel business located at 150 Quay Street, Rockhampton, Queensland, which offers hospitality services and accommodation. The Criterion Hotel Motel does not operate a bottle shop.
