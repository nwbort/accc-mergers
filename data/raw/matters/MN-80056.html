Cognizant Technology Solutions Australia Pty Ltd (Cognizant) proposes to acquire 100% of the issued share capital in Evolve Holdco Pty Ltd, the indirect holding company of DyFlex Pty Ltd (DyFlex) (the Acquisition).

Cognizant is an indirect subsidiary of Cognizant Technology Solutions Corporation, a global professional services company. Cognizant’s services include consulting, application development, systems integration, quality engineering and assurance, engineering research and development, application maintenance, infrastructure and security as well as business process services and automation. As part of providing these services, Cognizant has a small presence in Australia in respect of the supply of SAP-related services.

DyFlex is an Australian-founded IT services provider, specialising in the implementation and support of SAP solutions across Australia and New Zealand. 

SAP is best known for its enterprise resource planning (ERP) software which helps businesses to manage and integrate core business processes. SAP licences the ability to sell, build on, implement and manage its software to third parties.
