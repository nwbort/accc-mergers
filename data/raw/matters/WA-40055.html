Description

Sheffield TopCo Pty Ltd, a member of the group of companies trading as Sheffield Group (Sheffield), proposes to acquire the business and all of the assets of Champion Parts Pty Ltd

Sheffield TopCo Pty Ltd, a member of the group of companies trading as Sheffield Group (Sheffield), proposes to acquire the business and all of the assets of Champion Parts Pty Ltd, trading as Champion Fasteners (Champion Fasteners) (the Acquisition).

Sheffield is an Australian wholesaler of hand tools, abrasives and power tool accessories predominantly supplying industrial wholesale and retail customers.

Sheffield is majority-owned by Seidler Equity Australia II, LP (Seidler Australia), a private equity fund advised and/or managed by affiliates of Seidler Equity.

Seidler Equity is a private investment firm specialising in providing long-term equity capital to family-owned businesses across Australia with a presence in the United States. Within Australia, Seidler Equity holds interests in various other industries, including consumer products, professional services, and business services.

Champion Fasteners is an Australian wholesaler of fasteners, fittings and tools predominantly supplying automotive wholesale and retail customers.
