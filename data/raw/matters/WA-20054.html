Triton Bidco Limited plans to acquire 100% of the share capital of UK RT Transaction Holdco Ltd (UK RT Transaction Holdco), by way of share transfer (the Acquisition).

Triton Bidco Limited is a newly established special purpose company wholly owned by Exponent Private Equity Partners GP V, LP, an Irish ILP controlled by Exponent Private Equity GP of GP V Limited (Exponent GP). Exponent GP is ultimately controlled by Exponent Private Equity (Holdings) LLP (Exponent Holdings). Exponent Holdings, along with its subsidiaries and controlled entities (the Exponent Group) is an international private equity group based in the UK that invests in established family- and founder-led or corporate-owned businesses. In Australia, their investments span tourism, food, garden products, pharmaceuticals, chemicals, human resources technology, and industrial distribution.

The target, UK RT Transaction Holdco, is wholly owned by Crane Limited, a subsidiary ultimately controlled by Crane Company. The target trades as Crane Building Services & Utilities and supplies highly engineered flow-control solutions across water utilities, building services and gas utilities.

In Australia, the Target’s activities involve: (i) water utilities, specifically the sale of Viking Johnson-branded couplings, flange adaptors and related pipe-connection and repair products for water supply networks; (ii) building services, including sales of Crane branded valves used for Heating Ventilation and Airconditioning (HVAC) systems; and (iii) gas utilities, comprising sales of WASK branded fittings and safety equipment either directly to customers within Australia, or to third parties within the UK who supply such products to Australia.
