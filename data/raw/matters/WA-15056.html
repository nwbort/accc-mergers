INPEX Bonaparte CCS Pty Ltd (INPEX Bonaparte), an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of INPEX Corporation (INPEX), proposes to acquire a 21% participating interest in the Bonaparte carbon capture and storage (CCS) joint venture (Bonaparte CCS JV) from Woodside Energy Ltd (Woodside) (the Acquisition). 

The Bonaparte CCS JV is a contractual joint venture engaged in assessing the potential of establishing a CO2 storage facility within the G-7-AP block located off the northwest coast of the Northern Territory. Currently, the Bonaparte CCS JV is in the pre-final investment decision (pre-FID) phase. 

The current parties to the joint venture are: 

  • INPEX Browse E&P Pty Ltd, another indirect wholly owned subsidiary of INPEX, with a participating interest of 53%, which is the operator of the Bonaparte CCS JV; 

  • TotalEnergies CCS Australia Pty Ltd (TotalEnergies) (26% participating interest); and 

  • Woodside (21% participating interest), whose interest is the target of the  Acquisition. 

As a result of the Proposed Transaction, INPEX’s indirect participating interest in the Bonaparte CCS JV will increase to 74%.

INPEX is an oil and gas exploration and production company headquartered in Tokyo, Japan. It engages in research, exploration, development, production and sale of oil, natural gas, other mineral resources and in related businesses. INPEX is also engaged in lower-carbon solutions, including CCS, as well as hydrogen and integrated power supply. 

In addition to its interest in the Bonaparte CCS JV, INPEX has a minority interest in the Bayu-Undan CCS project, which is also pre-FID.
