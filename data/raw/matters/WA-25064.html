All-Lora Pty Ltd (All-Lora), a wholly owned subsidiary of Adcome Pty Ltd (together with its subsidiaries, Adcome), proposes to acquire the business and assets of the IGA Allora supermarket (the Target) from Hymac Aisles Pty Ltd as trustee for the Hymac Aisles Unit Trust (the Vendor).

Adcome operates a network of 35 supermarkets predominantly across regional Queensland, including under the IGA, Cornetts and Everfresh banners. Adcome is owned and controlled by Gray Trading Pty Ltd. Metcash Limited also has a non‑controlling shareholding in Adcome.

The Target is an independently owned IGA supermarket at 41-43 Warwick Street, Allora, a rural township in the Southern Downs Region of Queensland, approximately 25 kilometres north of Warwick and 60 kilometres south of Toowoomba. It is the only supermarket in the Allora township.

The acquisition will occur by way of an asset purchase. All-Lora will acquire the goodwill, plant and equipment, stock and other assets of the business and take an assignment of the existing lease of the supermarket premises. Following completion, All-Lora will continue to operate the store as an IGA supermarket. Adcome does not operate any supermarket in Allora or in the nearby towns of Clifton or Warwick.
