# Third-party material

Everything in this directory is a copy of material published by others,
mirrored so the pipeline can re-extract it and so the site can link to a
stable copy:

- matter pages and documents from the [ACCC acquisitions
  register](https://www.accc.gov.au/public-registers/mergers-and-acquisitions-registers/acquisitions-register),
  including determinations, questionnaires and notices written by the ACCC,
  and submissions and other documents written by merger parties and third
  parties;
- filings and decisions from the Australian Competition Tribunal, and
  orders and judgments of the Federal Court of Australia.

None of it is covered by this repository's licences: not the MIT licence
(`LICENSE`), which covers the code, and not CC BY 4.0 (`LICENSE-DATA`), which
covers this project's own data. Each document remains the property of its
owner and is subject to that owner's terms. Material from the ACCC website is
generally published by the ACCC under CC BY 4.0, but that does not extend to
documents written by other parties. Check the original source before reusing
any of it.

If you own material here and want it removed, please open an issue.
